1. Who we are and what this policy covers

BrainBox Life Skills Education Inc. is a company incorporated in British Columbia, Canada, that operates as BrainBox Educ ("BrainBox", "we", "us" or "our"). We publish BrainBox, a life-skills learning app for students aged 13 and up.

This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use the BrainBox app for iOS and Android (the "App"), our website at brainboxeduc.com, and the related services we provide (together, the "Service"), how we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights and choices you have.

We follow the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA). Our Privacy Officer is responsible for our compliance with this policy and with privacy law. You can reach the Privacy Officer at privacy@brainboxeduc.com.

This policy is part of our Terms of Use. Words defined there have the same meaning here.

2. What "personal information" means

Personal information is information about an identifiable individual. For BrainBox, that includes your email address, your nickname, your profile answers, and your learning progress while it is linked to your account. It does not include information that has been de-identified so that it can no longer reasonably be linked to you, or statistics that combine many users together.

3. The information we collect

We collect information in three ways: what you give us, what the App records as you learn, and what we receive from the app stores and our service providers.

3.1 Information you give us

  • Account. If you sign in by email, we collect your email address and send a 6-digit code to it each time you sign in. We never store a password for you. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple sends us a unique identifier and either your email address or a private relay address that forwards to you, depending on what you choose on Apple's screen. If Apple shares your name, we use it only as the display name on your profile, and only if you did not already choose one. We may add Google sign-in later. If we do, Google will share your email address and basic profile information when you choose that option, and we will update this policy first.
  • Profile. During onboarding we ask for a first name or nickname; an optional gender identity (Female, Male, Other, or Prefer not to say); an age range; how you heard about BrainBox; and your answers to two multiple-choice questions about your goals and the obstacles you face. These are stored on your learner profile.
  • Preferences. Your daily goal, your reminder preference, and the lessons you save.
  • Support. When you email us, we keep your email address and the contents of the conversation so we can help you and keep a record of what we did.

3.2 Information the App records as you learn

  • Lessons completed and skipped, quiz answers and scores, XP, streak, gems (BrainBucks) earned and spent, saved lessons, study time, and the courses and lessons you have unlocked.
  • When you redeem a class code, we record which code you used, when, and the access period it gives you.

3.3 Purchase information

  • When you buy a subscription or course inside the App, Apple or Google process the payment. We do not receive your card number or bank details. Through our purchase-management service, RevenueCat, we receive a record of what you bought, when, the price, the renewal or expiry date, and a store transaction identifier, so that we can unlock your content and keep it unlocked. RevenueCat also tells us which store you used and when the purchase was made or renewed, so we can keep your access correct.
  • When you buy on our website, our website platform provider, Thinkific, collects the information you enter on the order form under its own privacy policy. Thinkific shares the order details with us, including your email address and the course you bought, so that we can link the purchase to your App account. Thinkific sends us the email address on the order and the item purchased; it does not need to send us your name.

3.4 Technical information

  • Like any internet service, our servers and service providers receive your device's IP address, device type and operating system, App version, and the time of each request when the App connects to them. This information may be kept in logs for a limited time for security and troubleshooting.
  • The App does not currently contain any third-party analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software (SDKs). If we add one in the future, we will update this policy before it starts collecting information.

4. What we do not collect

We do not collect:

  • your full legal name (we ask only for a first name or nickname);
  • your date of birth (we ask only for an age range);
  • your phone number or home address;
  • photos, videos or voice recordings (the read-aloud feature plays an AI-generated voice and does not record you);
  • your precise location or your contacts;
  • behavioural data for advertising, or social media identifiers.

We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, device fingerprinting, or cross-app or cross-site trackers in the App.

5. How we use your information

We use personal information only for these purposes:

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in, including sending sign-in codes.
  • To provide the Service: track your progress, award XP, gems and streaks, unlock lessons and courses in order or with gems, remember your daily goal and reminder preference, and keep your progress available if you sign in on another device.
  • To confirm purchases and class codes and unlock what you have paid for or redeemed.
  • To communicate essentials: sign-in codes, changes to our Terms or this policy, warnings before we delete an inactive account, security alerts, and replies to your support requests.
  • To understand how BrainBox is used and improve it, using de-identified or aggregated information wherever possible. Your onboarding answers (age range, gender identity, how you heard about us, goals and obstacles) help us understand who uses BrainBox and what to build next. Your answers also shape the order in which the App suggests courses to you.
  • To keep the Service safe: detect cheating, fraud, abuse and unauthorized access, and enforce our Terms of Use.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as keeping tax and accounting records and responding to lawful requests.

We do not use your personal information for advertising, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

6. Consent

By creating an account and giving us information, you (or your parent or guardian, if you are a minor) consent to us collecting, using and sharing your personal information as described in this policy.

Some information is required to use the Service: your email address (or Apple sign-in), a nickname and an age range. Other information is optional, such as gender identity, where you can choose Prefer not to say.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by changing your settings, deleting your account, or contacting us (section 10). Withdrawing consent may mean we cannot provide some or all of the Service to you, and some uses may continue where the law requires or allows (for example, keeping purchase records). We will explain the consequences when you ask.

8. Where your information is stored

Our main database and sign-in service is provided by Supabase and hosted in Canada. Some of our other service providers process information in the United States. When your information is stored or processed outside Canada, it is protected by contract, but it may be subject to the laws of that country, including lawful access by courts, law enforcement and national security authorities there. You can ask us for more information about where our providers process data by emailing privacy@brainboxeduc.com.

9. Children, age and parents

BrainBox is designed for students aged 13 and older. During onboarding we ask for an age range, not a date of birth. We use your age range to apply this policy's protections for minors and to understand who uses BrainBox. We do not verify your age beyond what you tell us, and giving a false age breaks our Terms of Use.

If you are between 13 and the age of majority in your province or territory, your parent or guardian must agree to our Terms of Use and this policy on your behalf. Parents and guardians of minors can:

  • review the personal information we hold about their child;
  • ask us to correct it;
  • withdraw consent to its collection and use;
  • ask us to delete the child's account and data.

To do any of these, email privacy@brainboxeduc.com. We will take reasonable steps to confirm that you are the parent or guardian before we act, for example by confirming through the email address on the account. You can also use the in-app tools described in section 10.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it as quickly as we can. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, please email privacy@brainboxeduc.com.

Schools. When a school gives its students a class code, the school decides who receives the code. We do not require a school to send us student names or any other personal information to issue a code.

12. How we protect information

We use the following safeguards:

  • encryption of personal information in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest;
  • row-level access controls in our database, so that each account can read and change only its own records;
  • sign-in without passwords, using one-time codes sent to your email or Sign in with Apple, so there is no BrainBox password to steal;
  • access to production data limited to the people on our team who need it to do their jobs.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach of our security safeguards creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we will notify you and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as soon as feasible, as PIPEDA requires, and we will keep records of breaches as the law requires.

13. Reminders, local storage and tracking

Daily reminder. If you turn on the daily reminder, the App schedules it locally on your device. We do not collect a push notification token, and we do not send notifications from our servers at present. If we add server-sent notifications in the future, we will ask for your permission through your device and update this policy.

Local storage. The App stores a session token and some of your progress on your device so you can stay signed in and the App can work smoothly. Lesson content is downloaded to your device as you use the App, and progress you make while offline is sent to us when you reconnect. This information is removed when you delete the App.

No tracking. As section 4 says, the App contains no cookies, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or cross-app trackers, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. On iOS, we do not request App Tracking Transparency permission because we do not track you. Our website is run on Thinkific and may use cookies that are necessary for it to work.

14. Email from us

We send only the emails needed to run the Service: sign-in codes, account and security notices, changes to our Terms or this policy, warnings before we delete an inactive account, and replies to your requests. These are not marketing messages and you cannot opt out of them while you have an account.

We will not send you marketing email unless you have given express consent under Canada's anti-spam law. Every marketing message would include a way to unsubscribe.

15. If you are outside Canada

BrainBox is designed for Canada, and our content is written for Canadian law and life. If you use the Service from outside Canada, your information will be transferred to and processed in Canada and in the countries where our service providers operate (section 8). If you are outside Canada, you may have additional rights under local law, for example if you live in the European Union, the United Kingdom or the United States. You can exercise them by contacting us at privacy@brainboxeduc.com, and we will honour them where the law requires.

16. Changes to this policy

We will post updates to this policy here with a new "Last updated" date. If we make a material change (for example, collecting a new type of information, sharing information in a new way, or keeping it for a shorter or longer time), we will tell you at least 30 days before it takes effect, through a notice in the App and an email to the address on your account. Where the law requires, we will ask for your fresh consent, or your parent's or guardian's, before the change applies to you. Minor changes, such as clarifications or corrections, take effect when posted. Earlier versions are available on request.
