Common questions

  • I did not get my sign-in code. Check your spam folder for a message from no-reply@brainboxeduc.com, then request a new code in the app. Codes expire after 10 minutes.
  • I have a class or event code. On Android, enter it when you create your account, or later in Settings under "Redeem a class code". On iPhone, create your account in the app first, then enter the code on the web page from your handout or QR code, and reopen the app. Each code works once per account.
  • How do I cancel my subscription? Subscriptions are managed by the App Store or Google Play, not by us. On iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. On Android: Play Store, profile, Payments and subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date.
  • I changed phones. Sign in with the same email address and your progress follows you. Tap "Restore purchases" on the Premium screen to bring back a subscription.
  • How do I delete my account? In the app, open Settings and tap "Delete account". Your data is removed within 30 days.
  • How do I get a copy of my data? In the app, open Settings and tap "Download my data".
